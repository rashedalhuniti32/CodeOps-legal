Operational Realities

Why International Companies Struggle With Local Jordanian Operations

01

The Remote Workforce Hazard

Hiring local Jordanian developers or talent via foreign contractor agreements often invalidates IP assignment and triggers retroactive social security and labor liabilities under Jordanian Law.

02

The Bureaucratic Black Box

Cross-border powers of attorney, consular authentications, and corporate registry filings stall for months when handled by traditional law offices that lack digital tracking workflows.

03

The Data & Regulatory Trap

Jordan's Personal Data Protection Law (Law 24/2023) imposes strict compliance demands and cross-border transfer liabilities on foreign entities handling local user data.
